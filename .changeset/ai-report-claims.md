AI report drafts now work for every analysis source, not only Clash, Data validation, Compare and Load report. An answer can carry typed claims, and each claim is checked against the captured native values (exactly, or at the decimals a value written as a string states, with same-dimension unit conversion; row numbers written in the claim text count as citations) and labelled supported, unverifiable or contradicted. Contradicted claims block saving until a reviewer edits or removes them. You choose the narrative language for each draft. Text is made printable in the standard PDF fonts, with a notice for every substitution, so the preview and the PDF match. Generated text blocks record what the AI wrote, and the document embeds its captured evidence and claims.
