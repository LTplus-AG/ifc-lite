A saved AI report can refresh its evidence from **Documents**. It re-finds each cited row by its native identity, re-checks every claim, flags changed and missing values in the document, and regenerates untouched AI text. Human edits are kept unless the reviewer replaces them block by block, and deleted blocks stay deleted. The block list marks AI-generated and edited AI text. Duplicated blocks and documents built from an AI report as a template no longer carry its AI provenance or old evidence.
