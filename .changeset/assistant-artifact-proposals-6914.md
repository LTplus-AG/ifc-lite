The assistant can propose a filter, list, lens or chart as a reviewed native artifact. Every property, quantity and model name is checked against the loaded models first; a name the models do not carry exactly waits for the user to pick one of the real candidates. The proposal then runs through the tool's own engine and shows the matched population per model, units and measured/total denominators. Save writes an ordinary entry into saved filters, Lists, Lenses or a dashboard and opens the native panel; nothing selects, hides or colours the scene.
