Magnetic picking can snap CAD-style to the nearest feature corner on screen. With `snapOptions.screenCornerSnap` set (`{}` for the defaults), `raycastSceneMagnetic` snaps to the nearest corner of an eligible, visible, unclipped mesh whose projection is within `cornerRadiusPx` (10) of the cursor, even when the ray hits another element or nothing, and falls back to the nearest edge within `edgeRadiusPx` (6). Corners outrank edges. `eligibleModelIndices` restricts the search to the models allowed for the pick. Candidates hidden behind the ray-hit surface are skipped unless `ignoreOcclusion` is set, and the per-piece candidates and the screen grid are built in idle time (`requestIdleCallback`, on-screen pieces first) once the view has been still for `settleMs` (120), never inside a pick, so a pick only reads the grid and orbiting never stalls on it. Until the grid of the current view holds pieces the ray-based result stands. Without the option nothing changes.
