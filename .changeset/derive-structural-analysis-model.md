Derive a structural analysis model from the physical one (#9668). Beams, columns and members become curve members on their axis; slabs and walls become surface members on their mid-plane; member ends within a tolerance (default 0.05 m) share a node; a free member end moves along its own axis to meet another member's axis within a reach (default half that member's section plus the tolerance), and a member met in its interior is split, so beam-column joints on centroid axes become shared nodes; the report lists every moved end and every free end; footings and piles carry supports, or the nodes at the lowest level of the whole load-bearing structure do when no footing exists, flagged as guessed. Every member and node is linked to its physical element with `IfcRelAssignsToProduct`, and elements that already have a structural counterpart are skipped, so a second run writes nothing. `@ifc-lite/create` exports the three steps (`readStructuralDerivationFacts`, `planStructuralDerivation`, `applyStructuralDerivation`); `bim.store.planStructuralDerivation` and `bim.store.deriveStructuralAnalysisModel` are wired in the headless backend (`ifc-lite eval` and `run` scripts); the CLI adds `ifc-lite derive-structural`. A model that already has an analysis model is refused unless `whenAnalysisModelExists: 'add'` (CLI `--analysis-model-exists add`) derives the missing members into a new one. The plan carries that refusal (`plan.refusal`), so a caller can report it before applying. Apply checks the whole plan before its first write and writes in one atomic edit, so a refused plan writes nothing. Works on IFC2X3, IFC4 and IFC4X3. `@ifc-lite/parser` exports `effectiveStructuralView(store, view)`, the one adapter from an editable overlay to the structural read model, now shared by the viewer and the derivation. It also exports `effectiveStoreyResolver(store, context)`, which resolves the storeys of many products under one set of session edits without rebuilding the edited parent maps per product.
