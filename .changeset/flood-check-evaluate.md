`@ifc-lite/flood-check`, second part: the check against a surface-runoff hazard map. `checkFloodExposure` (pure) and `runFloodCheck` sample the hazard class on the ground next to each element the model reading found and compare every classified ground sample with its own terrain height and class. Outcomes: `exposed`, `open-ended-class`, `within-class-range`, `above-mapped-class-range` (only with a clearance margin derived from the terrain source's stated accuracy, the class resolution and a georeferencing term, plus an allowance for the highest ground-storey door above its terrain), `no-runoff-mapped` (worded as not meaning safe, and only with a complete source of other buildings' footprints) and `unclassified`. The two reassuring outcomes are withheld under one shared list of conditions (no stated terrain accuracy, upper-estimate level, unreadable, off-raster, out-of-coverage or excluded samples, moved anchors, hull, partial or courtyard footprints, an unconfirmed height reference, an unconfirmed rendering layer, other buildings unknown). Every result carries the deciding sample and its numbers, what the outcome means, the elements skipped, and a screening caveat with attribution and dataset date. Refuses mismatched or unrecognised vertical CRSs, a ground storey implausible against the terrain along the whole footprint outline, and a plan extent no building has. Ships a provider for the Swiss BAFU "Gefährdungskarte Oberflächenabfluss" (WMS GetMap with exact legend-colour decoding, the Swiss and Liechtenstein coverage perimeter, a rendering check), terrain grids with an explicit node or cell-centre convention, the publisher's German and English disclaimer, and `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffHazardSite` / `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffExposure` property-set payloads for a later writer.
