Add `@ifc-lite/flood-check`, first part: reading a georeferenced building model for a surface-runoff screening. `extractModelFacts` reads, from a parsed model and without meshing, the elements such a check needs: doors, windows, openings, light wells, external stairs and ramps, and technical equipment in the lowest storeys (IFC2X3 equipment through its type objects). Each comes with its map position and level, read in one place (`locate`) from placements, own bodies and the openings it fills, with the quality of both: a level that is only an upper estimate, or a position that cannot be proven, is marked so, never passed off as exact. The building footprint comes from a given outline, the ground-storey slabs or the external walls, with a quality record that only accumulates through every fallback (filled voids, oversized, courtyard rings, partial, hull) and one outline rule for seams and narrow gaps between slabs. Every geometry reader reports what it could not read. Candidate selection is one walk with one verdict per element: walked = candidates + skipped, with a reason for every skip. Refuses missing or implausible georeferencing, unrecognised or mismatched CRSs (EPSG codes, OGC URIs, WKT), a shifted WorldCoordinateSystem, ambiguous map scales and an ambiguous building; a ground storey not stated by the caller is recorded as a guess. The hazard-map check, the Swiss BAFU provider and the property-set payloads follow in the next release.
