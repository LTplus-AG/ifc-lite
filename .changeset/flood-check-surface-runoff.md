'@ifc-lite/flood-check': minor

Add `@ifc-lite/flood-check`: a headless screening of a georeferenced building model against a surface-runoff hazard map. It reads doors, windows, openings, light wells, external stairs and ramps, and technical equipment in the lowest storeys from a parsed model (no meshing; IFC2X3 equipment through its type objects), samples the hazard class on the ground next to each, and compares every classified ground sample with its own terrain height and class. Outcomes: `exposed`, `open-ended-class`, `within-class-range`, `above-mapped-class-range` (only with a clearance margin derived from the terrain source's stated accuracy, the class resolution and a georeferencing term that defaults to an unsurveyed 0.5 m, plus an allowance for the height of the lowest ground-storey door above its terrain), `no-runoff-mapped` (worded as not meaning safe) and `unclassified`. The two reassuring outcomes are withheld under one shared list of conditions (no stated terrain accuracy, upper-estimate level, unreadable, off-raster, out-of-coverage or excluded samples, moved anchors, hull footprints, an unconfirmed height reference, an unconfirmed rendering layer). Every result carries the deciding sample and its numbers, what the outcome means, and a screening caveat with attribution and dataset date. Refuses missing or mismatched georeferencing, unrecognised or mismatched vertical CRSs (compared by EPSG code), a ground storey implausible against the terrain sampled along the whole footprint outline, and a plan extent no building has. The model's height reference cannot be verified from the hazard map: favourable outcomes need every ground-storey door to sit within 2.5 m of the terrain next to it, and every result states the height error the run cannot detect. No option makes an outcome more favourable than the default except by supplying information; tolerances can only be tightened. Ships a provider for the Swiss BAFU "Gefährdungskarte Oberflächenabfluss" (WMS GetMap with exact legend-colour decoding, Swiss and Liechtenstein coverage perimeter, a one-request rendering check), terrain grids with an explicit node or cell-centre convention, the publisher's German and English disclaimer, and `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffHazardSite` / `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffExposure` property-set payloads for a later writer.
