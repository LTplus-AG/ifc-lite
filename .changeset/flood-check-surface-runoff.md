Add `@ifc-lite/flood-check`: a headless screening of a georeferenced building model against a surface-runoff hazard map. It reads doors, windows, openings, light wells, external stairs and ramps, and technical equipment in the lowest storeys from a parsed model (no meshing; IFC2X3 equipment through its type objects), samples the hazard class on the ground next to each, and compares every classified ground sample with its own terrain height and class. Outcomes: `exposed`, `open-ended-class`, `within-class-range`, `above-mapped-class-range` (only with a clearance margin derived from the terrain source's stated accuracy, the class resolution and georeferencing, and withheld for hull footprints, moved anchors, unreadable samples and upper-estimate levels), `no-runoff-mapped` (only inside the provider's coverage perimeter and with a confirmed rendering layer; worded as not meaning safe) and `unclassified`, each with the deciding sample and its numbers, what the outcome means, and a screening caveat with attribution and dataset date. Refuses missing or mismatched georeferencing, unrecognised or mismatched vertical CRSs (compared by EPSG code), a ground storey implausible against the sampled terrain, and a plan extent no building has. Ships a provider for the Swiss BAFU "Gefährdungskarte Oberflächenabfluss" (WMS GetMap with exact legend-colour decoding, Swiss and Liechtenstein coverage perimeter, a one-request rendering check), terrain grids with an explicit node or cell-centre convention, the publisher's German and English disclaimer, and `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffHazardSite` / `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffExposure` property-set payloads for a later writer.
