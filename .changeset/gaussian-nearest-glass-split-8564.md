The internal, unqualified Gaussian adapter composes splats with transparent IFC by a nearest-glass split: splats behind the nearest transparent layer draw before it, the rest after it, per sample. This is exact where a sample has at most one transparent layer, and for splats behind or in front of every layer; splats between two layers stay approximate. It covers perspective and orthographic views, 1x and 4x MSAA, and every transparent draw path (including X-Ray ghosts). Frames without transparent IFC keep the single splat pass, and frames without visible splats record nothing extra. `GaussianSplatStats.composition` now reports the last splat frame (`nearest-glass-split`, `no-transparent`, or `over-transparent-approximate` with a fallback reason). Details are in the new `compositionReport`, including whether a sample had two or more transparent layers. `gaussianSplats.setCompositionMode('single-pass')` keeps the old pass. Readiness stays `blocked`: the blocker tuple is unchanged, and the new capability is `nearest-glass-split`. Exports `GaussianComposition`, `GaussianCompositionFallback`, `GaussianCompositionMode`, `GaussianCompositionReport` and `GaussianTransparentLayers`.
