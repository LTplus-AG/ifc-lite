Void cuts stay closed in more cases: a consolidated host that sliver cleanup or the rim weld used to tear, a prism cut through cleanup and consolidation (kept even where consolidation cannot keep it closed), a cut section welded the way the half-space cap is, and a cut chipped by a touching opening, which is now retried. An opening is bounded by its placed vertices. A sliver is split only when the split improves the result, and a narrow face whose short edge lies inside its region is kept.
