Storey levels drive the elements that depend on them (#9296). Columns (grid-bound ones included), beams, members and curtain walls now follow their top attachment through a storey edit, like walls: a column takes the new height, a level beam rises with the storey above, a brace keeps its foot and reaches the new level, and a curtain wall is laid out again at the new height with every part kept. A railing that runs up a refit stair is stretched with it. A wall's top can attach to a slab's underside and follows the slab's storey. `IfcLite_StoreyAttachment.TopBinding` records whether a storey attachment is relative ("home storey + 1": an inserted storey re-binds it, a copy to another storey attaches to the storey above its new one) or explicit (it stays on its storey); records written before it read as relative when they name the next storey above, explicit otherwise. New: `setTopConstraintInStore` (`bim.store.setTopConstraint`), `planStoreyAdd` (`bim.store.planAddStorey`), `copyToStoreysInStore` (`bim.store.copyToStoreys`, one undoable copy to several storeys, results per storey), and a `TopConstraint` option on `bim.store.addWall`, `addColumn`, `addColumnOnGrid`, `addBeam`, `addMember` and `addCurtainWall`, whose `auto` default attaches an element drawn up to the storey above. Elements with no attachment record keep their height.
