Measure gains a Point mode next to Distance, Polyline, Angle and Radius: one click (or tap) on the model reads that point's Model X/Y/Z, with no distance gesture and no georeferencing needed. A compact HUD line shows the hovered or placed point, and the Measurements panel's Point tab shows the full frame stack (Render when shifted, Datum/Relative with a reference, Map/LatLon with Geo XYZ on and a usable georef). The point is a transient reading: Esc or leaving Point mode drops it (closing the tool keeps it readable in the Point tab), and it is not listed, counted or exported as a measurement.
