Measure: with Snap off, holding Shift during a Distance measurement no longer snaps to edges or vertices (#8995). Shift still keeps the rubber band and the finishing click on the axis, but the edge lock and the snap glyph no longer come back while Snap is off. With Snap on, Shift snaps as before. All Measure picks now share one set of snap options.
