Convert qualified positive-pattern dashed PDF strokes on open straight subpaths into styled IFC annotation fill geometry. Dash phase, odd-pattern repetition, per-subpath reset, vertex joins, caps and complete affine calibration are preserved; closed, curved and zero-containing dash forms remain explicit fidelity-report omissions. Combined fill and dashed-stroke paints still refuse atomically when multiple dash-run boundaries create crossings outside the current fill-region qualifier.
