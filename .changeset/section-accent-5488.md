The section tool's on-canvas marks now follow the viewer theme (#5488). The axis badge, the face-picked plane's drag arrow and the hover preview are all drawn in the one interaction accent, for every axis and for face-picked planes alike, matching the tinted plane in the 3D view. The per-axis cyan, green and orange and the violet used for face-picked cuts are gone; which axis a cut runs along is shown by a small axis-coloured dot on the badge.
