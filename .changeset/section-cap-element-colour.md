Section caps can fill with each element's own colour (new `fillMode: 'element'` cap style; `DrawingPolygon.sourceColor` carries the source sub-mesh colour on every cut polygon), and a selected or hovered element's cap polygons are drawn in the highlight colour via the new `RenderOptions.capHighlight`.
