Every document block with a heading (text, image, chart, BCF topic, table, IDS or information validation report, manual validation report) now has the same heading controls beside its title: a size from 6 to 24 pt, a text colour and a background colour. They are drawn by the preview and the PDF from one shared reading, scale with the block's size, reserve their extra height so nothing leaves the printable frame, and are kept when a report block is refreshed, replaced from a saved report or filled from a workflow template. With only a background set, the heading ink is black or white by contrast. Documents without these fields print as before.
