**diff**: add opt-in **successor claims** to `diffModels` (issue #4955) — one deleted entity replaced in place by one added entity whose data AND geometry both changed: a wall with a new buildup, a chair from a different family. Content matching needs a hash to agree and split/merge needs volumes to conserve; this last stage argues from position alone, on whatever those two left unbound, and is therefore a suggestion provider and nothing more: a `SuccessorClaim` on `ModelDiff.successors` retires nothing, touches no count, and never becomes an identity-map entry unless a caller passes it to the new `identityMapFromSuccessors` as accepted (reason `successor:footprint` / `successor:position`; a `here` two accepted claims disagree about yields nothing). Two named profiles, each requiring a pairing unique in both directions with a margin: `footprint` (bounding-box intersection over union at or above `successorOverlap`, default 0.6, nothing else on either side within half of it) and `position` (same class family, same new optional `EntityFingerprint.container` name path, mutual nearest within `max(successorDistance, 0.5 × base diagonal)` with the runner-up at least twice as far). `mutualNearestPairs` gains an optional margin ratio, default 1 so the content pass is byte-identical. Also exports `ACCEPTED_AMBIGUOUS_REASON` for a review UI minting identity out of an `ambiguous` group.
