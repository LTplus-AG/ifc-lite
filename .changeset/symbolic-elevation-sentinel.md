Say "no elevation" with `NaN` in the symbolic extractor, so a symbol at world Y = 0 is a symbol at ground level.

Every symbolic primitive's `worldY` is summed from at most two sources: the Z translation accumulated along the product's placement chain, and the Z ordinate of the primitive's own geometry point. Both are genuinely optional in real files — an `IfcAxis2Placement2D` chain has no Z at all, an `IfcCartesianPoint` may be authored with two ordinates, and a product may carry no `ObjectPlacement` whatsoever — and each missing source used to default to `0.0`. That collapsed "at datum" and "this file never said" onto the same number, and no consumer could tell them apart afterwards: the viewer's storey bucketing, which falls back to the storey table when the extractor resolves no elevation, was reduced to guessing with `worldY !== 0`, so every ground-floor annotation and grid axis was read as unresolved. It was re-bucketed onto whatever elevation the storey table claimed for its element, and in the storey-less exports that fallback exists to serve (3DEXPERIENCE / IfcPlusPlus, where the spatial hierarchy cannot be built at all) it was dropped into the loose bucket and drawn at the model's mid-height instead of on the ground. Ground floors at 0.0 are the common case, not a corner: in this repo's own fixtures that is 90 of 90 symbolic primitives in one and 190 of 2470 in another.

An unresolved `worldY` now arrives as `NaN` from all four producer paths (polylines and arcs, circles, text literals, fill areas) plus grid axes, so `Number.isFinite` alone separates the two and `0` keeps its authored meaning. `NaN` is the sentinel these types already documented, and it is preserved end to end — a `Float32Array` carries it across the worker boundary and `nan_as_null` already spells it `null` on the server's JSON wire, where `0` and `null` stay distinct. Elevations that were never ambiguous are untouched, negative (basement) elevations included.

The same pass corrects a translation the extractor was silently dropping. `IfcCartesianTransformationOperator2D`/`3D` (an `IfcMappedItem`'s MappingTarget) already read `LocalOrigin` for the plan translation but stopped at the second ordinate, so an authored Z went nowhere: a target at Z = 5 over a MappingOrigin at Z = 0 came out at 0, and over a product placed at Z = 7 it came out at 7 rather than 12. That was masked while every missing contribution defaulted to `0.0` and the consumer treated `0` as unresolved; under the sentinel the same symbol would instead be asserted to sit at ground level. The third ordinate is now read through the same `point_elevation` helper, so a two-ordinate `LocalOrigin` (a 2D operator) still contributes nothing rather than a fabricated datum.
