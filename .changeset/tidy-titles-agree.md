A BCF topic's own title is now cut to the text column, in the preview and the PDF, instead of running into the snapshot or off the page. A topic that is not loaded is headed "BCF topic <guid>" and keeps its full not-loaded notice as a line below, so a large heading never cuts the notice away. A chart's heading strip spans the same width as every other block's. The three heading controls are now named "Title size", "Title colour" and "Title background", apart from the table's "Header background" and "Header text".
