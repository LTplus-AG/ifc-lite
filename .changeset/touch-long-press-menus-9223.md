Touch long press and mouse menus. A trackpad or mouse right-click next to a finger that is still down now stops that finger's hold, so its long-press menu no longer replaces the one you asked for. This also holds when the right button went down before the finger landed. On Windows, lifting a long press held for more than about a second and a half no longer opens the menu a second time.
