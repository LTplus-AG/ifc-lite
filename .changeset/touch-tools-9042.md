A finger, and an Apple Pencil on iPad, now does what a mouse does in three tools. A tap with Section face-pick armed places the cut through the tapped face (a tap on empty space disarms the pick, as a click does). A tap in the command tool places the command's point. A one-finger drag pans in the Pan tool and looks around in the Walk tool, where it used to orbit. Pinch and two-finger pan are unchanged.
