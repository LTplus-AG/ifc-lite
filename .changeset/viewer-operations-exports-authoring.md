Agents connected through the local bridge can export, author and validate through native viewer operations: STEP/IFCX/LandXML/GLB/anonymized and changed-model exports as host-owned artifacts, element authoring with prepare/apply/discard candidates, property edits, storeys, zones, change history and IDS Studio/validation. Each operation uses the same producer or transaction as its panel, and a native refusal is never reported as a committed write. The panels behave as before.
