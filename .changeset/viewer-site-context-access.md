Site context and the surface runoff check are on the ribbon (View › Context) as well as the palette (#10603). Both open the model's Information panel and bring their section into view; for a model that cannot host them yet (no georeferencing, a CRS or height datum that does not resolve, a site outside Switzerland) the section says what is missing and how to fix it, instead of disappearing.
