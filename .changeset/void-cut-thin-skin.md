A recess that stops less than a millimetre short of the far face of its host keeps the skin behind it. An 11 mm recess in a 12 mm finish layer used to be pushed through the remaining 1 mm. Two rules of the void cut read a host face within about a millimetre of a cutter cap as lying on it, and a third missed a recess that starts on a face the facet weld had rounded to the kernel grid. All three now take their bounds from the grid (61 um for the first two, half a step for the third). On a Revit wall this returns 0.140 m2 of surface and 70 cm3.
