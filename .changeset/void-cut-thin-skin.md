A recess that stops less than a millimetre short of the far face of its host keeps the skin behind it. An 11 mm recess in a 12 mm finish layer used to be pushed through the remaining 1 mm, because three rules of the void cut each read a host face within about a millimetre of a cutter cap as lying on it. They now share one bound of four steps of the kernel grid (61 um). On a Revit wall this returns 0.140 m2 of surface and 70 cm3.
