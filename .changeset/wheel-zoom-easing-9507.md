Wheel zoom in the 3D view, the Drawing panel and plans now eases in over about 0.2 s instead of jumping, and a fast spin or trackpad momentum can no longer pile up into a runaway zoom. Trackpad scrolls and pinches still follow the fingers directly, reversing the wheel stops the zoom, and the system's reduced-motion setting applies each notch at once. A new **Wheel zoom speed** setting (Slow, Normal, Fast) in Settings → Display → Navigation applies to the 3D view, drawings and plans. Line- and page-mode wheels (some Firefox setups) now zoom as far as pixel-mode wheels in every view.
