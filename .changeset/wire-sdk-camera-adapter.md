"@ifc-lite/viewer": patch

Wire `bim.viewer.getCamera()`, `setCamera()`, and `flyTo()` to the live renderer camera instead of ignoring the data flowing through them. `getCamera()` now returns real `position`/`target`/`up`, not just `mode` — the SDK's own documented `bim.bcf.createViewpoint({ camera: bim.viewer.getCamera() })` pattern previously always built a positionless viewpoint (#4264). `setCamera(state)` now applies `position`/`target`/`up` in addition to `mode`, which it silently dropped before. `flyTo(refs)` was a complete no-op — it did not even accept a `refs` parameter, despite an inline comment claiming it was "wired via useBimHost" (it was not) — and now resolves refs to global ids the same way `colorize()` does, expanding a geometry-less assembly ref to its parts (#3338), and frames the camera on them via `cameraCallbacks.frameEntities` (#4266). All three go through the same `cameraCallbacks.getViewpoint`/`applyViewpoint`/`frameEntities` non-React entry points `lib/tours/snapshot.ts` and `SearchModal.filter.tsx` already use — no new coordinate convention introduced. The headless `packages/mcp`/`packages/cli` backends and `packages/viewer`'s streaming-viewer stub are untouched; they remain correctly no-op with no renderer to draw from.
