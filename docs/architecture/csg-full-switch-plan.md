## Critical risks

- [CRITICAL] f32-near-coplanar faces are the DOMINANT real degeneracy and the exact predicate gets them wrong-by-design (regresses vs Manifold). Real IFC is authored in f32; an intended-flush opening/wall face has orient3d≠Zero after quantization, so classify_vs_plane returns Crosses/Disjoint and the coplanar path is NEVER entered → the kernel emits a noise-sliver or a collapsed None interval where Manifold (merge-epsilon) cut cleanly. Exactness is actively harmful here. Mitigation: M3a exact snap-rounding in mesh_to_tris (shared bit-deterministic grid). Residual risk if the grid is mis-sized → either uncut (too coarse) or un-snapped (too fine); must be tuned against the M7 corpus.
- [CRITICAL] Panic in the production hot path is unrecoverable on wasm (panic='abort') and aborts the worker — strictly worse than today's silent un-cut. Six confirmed reachable sites (retriangulate.rs:298/322/385, arrangement.rs:229 to_f64().unwrap() AHEAD of any budget, mesh_bridge.rs:18-20 unchecked index, rational.rs:15 non-finite). catch_unwind only works server-side (profile.server-release). Mitigation: M6 panic-free-by-construction as a HARD gate (rewrite all 6 to Option/Result) + server catch_unwind + refutation_fuzz. Residual: a panic site outside the enumerated 6 surfacing on a corpus model outside the harness.
