opening repair `ad07f72a56`. No production region seam is implemented by that
repair. The actual #3641 model remains a required preservation target; its retained
positive SDK/Worker witness stays red at source admission. A negative refusal

## Establish the specific source and derived operation

Keep `#61272` / `#61273` / `#61274` and their authored order `(1,2,3,4,5,1)`
unchanged. Keep original source records, f64 coefficient bits, model epoch/revision,
raw millimetre units, 0.2 mm context Precision, extrusion Position, DirectionRatios,

The original chain continues to fail simple-profile admission. Do not change its
status to Complete or replace its source records with a different polygon.

The production source capture must retain an owned raw profile-chain graph before
simple-region validation, under the same reached ItemSource and source owner.
Today profile validation clears rejected loops. Factor raw capture from admission
so the rejected original remains available as an authenticated input, with its
reports and unsupported-simple-profile fact intact. Reuse canonical curve/source
extraction; add no source walker. Selection must use a typed admissibility result,
never substring matching of the current error message.

Add explicit logical region operations and outputs rather than disguising a
rewritten profile as an authored Extrusion. Proposed contracts:

- `AuthoredProfileChain`: immutable source/probe result, not an admitted region or
  numerical solid. Binds source owner, profile/curve/point records, original ordered
  directed edges, units, Precision, raw coefficient bits and occurrence slot.
- `RegularizeRegion`: derived operation over that exact chain, with a versioned
  cancellation/region policy and complete edge-range decomposition. It yields a
  distinct `LogicalRetainedRegion` slot and an authenticated topology certificate.
- `ExtrudeRegion`: derived extrusion over that sealed region and the original
  extrusion source. Binds original Position, DirectionRatios, Depth and source /
  evaluation frames; its output is the ordinary logical solid consumed by existing
  Affine/Boolean/opening continuations. A region-only result cannot satisfy a solid
  dependency or final product manifest.

Cache keys include original source identity/epoch/revision, original coefficient
bits, region policy/version, units/Precision and occurrence semantics. A reusable
source definition/certificate may be shared; mapped occurrences retain distinct
slots and ordered frames. The simple-profile branch and exact default are preserved.
No generic UnsupportedSource recovery may silently select this operation.
