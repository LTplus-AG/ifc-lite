# LTplus AG model report confidentiality agreement draft

**Draft for owner and legal review. Not approved for automatic issuance.** The production service must not enable this agreement merely because this file exists.

## Parties and report

The recipient is **LTplus AG, Bolligenstrasse 24A, 3006 Bern, Switzerland**, represented by **Louis Trümpler, founder**, contact **info@lt.plus** (LTplus). The disclosing party is the person or organisation identified in the acceptance record (Discloser). The record identifies the representative, report reference, agreement version, exact text digest and acceptance time. Each representative confirms authority to act for the identified party.

## Purpose and confidential material

LTplus may use privately submitted models, drawings, screenshots, associated data and information derived from them only to reproduce, diagnose, correct and verify the IFC Lite problem identified by the report. These materials are Confidential Material whether or not individually marked confidential. This agreement also covers materials subsequently attached privately to that same report.

LTplus will not use Confidential Material for unrelated product research, demonstrations, marketing, public benchmarks, machine learning training or external AI services without the Discloser's separate, specific permission. LTplus may publish a software fix and a general description of the defect only where neither reveals Confidential Material or enables its reconstruction.

## Protection and access

LTplus will protect Confidential Material using reasonable technical and organisational measures, with at least the care it applies to comparable confidential information of its own. Access is restricted to authorised personnel and contracted service providers who need it for the stated purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these terms. LTplus remains responsible for their compliance.

The service must identify its hosting locations and relevant processing providers before upload. LTplus being a Swiss company is not a promise that all storage or processing occurs in Switzerland. Material must not be transferred to an undisclosed processing location or provider without notifying the Discloser and obtaining any permission required by the agreed upload conditions and applicable law.

Private files, signer details and private download credentials will not be included in public GitHub issues. The Discloser separately reviews any report text or diagnostic information proposed for public posting. Publishing a particular file requires a separate explicit instruction identifying that file; such permission does not release other private materials from this agreement.

## Exceptions and required disclosures

Confidentiality does not apply to information that LTplus can demonstrate was already lawfully known without restriction, becomes public without a breach, is independently developed without using Confidential Material, or is lawfully obtained from a third party without a confidentiality restriction.

If disclosure is legally required, LTplus may disclose only what is required and, where legally permitted, will give prompt notice so the Discloser may seek protection. Nothing restricts disclosures or rights that applicable law does not allow the parties to restrict.

## Ownership and authority to share

The Discloser retains its rights in the materials. LTplus receives only the limited permission needed for the stated purpose; this agreement transfers no ownership or broader licence. The Discloser confirms that it is authorised to share the materials and requests that unnecessary personal or third-party confidential information be removed before submission. This agreement does not replace any separately required data processing agreement.

## Retention and deletion

Unless the Discloser explicitly agrees to an extension, uploaded Confidential Material and derived debugging copies, extracts and screenshots will be deleted from active storage within 30 days of upload, or within seven days of an earlier deletion request. LTplus will require authorised recipients to delete their working copies within the same period. Access is revoked promptly when deletion is requested. Any backup retention and deletion schedule must be disclosed before upload and must also cover these materials; residual backup copies remain protected and may not be used for another purpose.

LTplus may retain a minimal agreement and deletion audit record, without model contents, only as necessary to establish compliance or satisfy legal obligations. Any material retained because law requires it remains restricted to that obligation. Suspected unauthorised access or disclosure will be investigated promptly and the Discloser notified without undue delay.

## Duration and electronic acceptance

The obligations begin when the service records acceptance of this exact, approved version under LTplus's standing authorisation to issue it. They continue for five years after each disclosure; material qualifying as a protected trade secret remains protected for as long as that status continues under applicable law.

The parties agree to conclude this standard agreement electronically through the service's review and acceptance process without requiring a handwritten signature. The service supplies a dated, downloadable agreement and acceptance record. This record must accurately describe electronic issuance and acceptance and must not claim a qualified electronic signature unless one was actually used. A customer-provided NDA or any alteration of these terms requires separate review and cannot be accepted automatically.

## Governing law and disputes

Swiss substantive law governs this agreement. Subject to mandatory applicable jurisdiction rules, disputes are submitted to the competent courts at LTplus AG's registered seat in Switzerland. Either party may seek urgent protective relief from a competent court.
