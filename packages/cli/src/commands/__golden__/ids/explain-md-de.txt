# Door fire ratings (rev B)

Every door states its fire rating.

## Fire rating on all doors (required, IFC4)

Doors carry a fire rating.

**Applies to**

- Elemente vom Typ "IFCDOOR"

**Requires**

- Optional: Eigenschaft ""Pset_DoorCommon"."FireRating"" muss existieren
- Attribut ""Name"" muss existieren

> Fill Pset_DoorCommon.FireRating.

## Windows carry a thermal transmittance (optional, IFC4)

**Applies to**

- Elemente vom Typ "IFCWINDOW"

**Requires**

- Eigenschaft ""Pset_WindowCommon"."ThermalTransmittance"" muss existieren
