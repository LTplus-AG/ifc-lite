## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [#10486](https://github.com/LTplus-AG/ifc-lite/pull/10486) [`2d09a32`](https://github.com/LTplus-AG/ifc-lite/commit/2d09a32fcea4035b878c5d2b62ccef413fe3ecae) Thanks [@louistrue](https://github.com/louistrue)! - `@ifc-lite/flood-check`, second part: the check against a surface-runoff hazard map. `checkFloodExposure` (pure) and `runFloodCheck` sample the hazard class on the ground next to each element the model reading found and compare every classified ground sample with its own terrain height and class. Outcomes: `exposed`, `open-ended-class`, `within-class-range`, `above-mapped-class-range` (only with a clearance margin derived from the terrain source's stated accuracy, the class resolution and a georeferencing term, plus an allowance for the highest ground-storey door above its terrain), `no-runoff-mapped` (worded as not meaning safe, and only with a complete source of other buildings' footprints) and `unclassified`. The two reassuring outcomes are withheld under one shared list of conditions (no stated terrain accuracy, upper-estimate level, unreadable, off-raster, out-of-coverage or excluded samples, moved anchors, hull, partial or courtyard footprints, an unconfirmed height reference, an unconfirmed rendering layer, other buildings unknown). Every result carries the deciding sample and its numbers, what the outcome means, the elements skipped, and a screening caveat with attribution and dataset date. Refuses mismatched or unrecognised vertical CRSs, a ground storey implausible against the terrain along the whole footprint outline, and a plan extent no building has. Ships a provider for the Swiss BAFU "Gefährdungskarte Oberflächenabfluss" (WMS GetMap with exact legend-colour decoding, the Swiss and Liechtenstein coverage perimeter, a rendering check), terrain grids with an explicit node or cell-centre convention, the publisher's German and English disclaimer, and `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffHazardSite` / `IfcLite_SurfaceRunoffExposure` property-set payloads for a later writer.

- [#10403](https://github.com/LTplus-AG/ifc-lite/pull/10403) [`d021907`](https://github.com/LTplus-AG/ifc-lite/commit/d021907e000d8150eba3f8b7306f2f6e967f1b62) Thanks [@louistrue](https://github.com/louistrue)! - Add `@ifc-lite/flood-check`, first part: reading a georeferenced building model for a surface-runoff screening. `extractModelFacts` reads, from a parsed model and without meshing, the elements such a check needs: doors, windows, openings, light wells, external stairs and ramps, and technical equipment in the lowest storeys (IFC2X3 equipment through its type objects). Each comes with its map position and level, read in one place (`locate`) from placements, own bodies and the openings it fills, with the quality of both: a level that is only an upper estimate, or a position that cannot be proven, is marked so, never passed off as exact. The building footprint comes from a given outline, the ground-storey slabs or the external walls, with a quality record that only accumulates through every fallback (filled voids, oversized, courtyard rings, partial, hull) and one outline rule for seams and narrow gaps between slabs. Every geometry reader reports what it could not read. Candidate selection is one walk with one verdict per element: walked = candidates + skipped, with a reason for every skip. Refuses missing or implausible georeferencing, unrecognised or mismatched CRSs (EPSG codes, OGC URIs, WKT), a shifted WorldCoordinateSystem, ambiguous map scales and an ambiguous building; a ground storey not stated by the caller is recorded as a guess. The hazard-map check, the Swiss BAFU provider and the property-set payloads follow in the next release.

### Patch Changes

- [#10503](https://github.com/LTplus-AG/ifc-lite/pull/10503) [`d3a200a`](https://github.com/LTplus-AG/ifc-lite/commit/d3a200a74f6b6c354e3647c088830f045e75b6a2) Thanks [@louistrue](https://github.com/louistrue)! - `@ifc-lite/flood-check` follow-up. Bodies given as `IfcMappedItem` (how Revit exports doors, windows and equipment) are now read through their `RepresentationMap`, placed by `MappingTarget · MappingOrigin`, with nested maps followed (depth and cycle guarded) and non-uniform scales refused as unreadable; on a Revit model this turns upper-estimate levels and uncertain positions into exact ones. A slab hole cut by a Boolean DIFFERENCE through the whole slab is read and classified like a profile hole instead of withholding every favourable outcome for the building, and a hole counts as roofed by roofs, roofing coverings, skylights and plates as well as slabs (over its whole area). A clipped body's bottom equal to the placement level is labelled a lower bound, and with no footprint the outside test answers "unknown". Fetched test fixtures fail the test run in CI when absent instead of skipping silently.
